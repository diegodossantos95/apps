Margin

The reader's companion. Turn any article link into a concise AI summary you can read — or listen to, hands-free.

What it does

Paste a link and Margin delivers a concise AI summary in your language, ready to play aloud while you commute, cook, walk, or work. Summarize directly from Safari, Apple News, Mail, or any app via the share sheet.

Highlights

  • AI summaries with live web access — even today's news.
  • Automatic translation to your device language.
  • High-quality text-to-speech with adjustable speed.
  • Share extension for one-tap summaries from any app.
  • Local summary library, searchable and offline-friendly.
